## Types of Land Freight Solutions

Every shipment has different transportation requirements. At Maxline Global, we offer customized road freight solutions designed to meet your business needs.

### Full Truck Load (FTL)
FTL shipping is ideal for businesses transporting large quantities of cargo that require an entire truck.

Benefits include:
- Faster delivery
- Reduced cargo handling
- Higher security
- Lower risk of damage

### Less Than Truck Load (LTL)
LTL allows multiple shipments from different customers to share truck space, reducing transportation costs for smaller loads.

Advantages include:
- Affordable shipping
- Flexible scheduling
- Better vehicle utilization
- Reduced logistics expenses

### Door-to-Door Delivery
Door-to-door transportation simplifies logistics by handling pickup, transportation, customs coordination, and final delivery under one service.

### Cross-Border GCC Transportation
Our experienced logistics professionals ensure smooth cargo movement between the UAE and GCC countries through efficient customs documentation and route planning.
