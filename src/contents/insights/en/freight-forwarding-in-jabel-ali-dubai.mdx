### Sea Freight Forwarding Services in Dubai

As home to the largest port in the Middle East, Jebel Ali is a major gateway for international maritime trade. Sea freight forwarding in Dubai remains one of the most cost-effective solutions for transporting large volumes of cargo globally.

#### Full Container Load (FCL)

FCL shipping is ideal for businesses transporting large quantities of goods. Dedicated container space ensures greater security, reduced handling, and faster transit times.

#### Less than Container Load (LCL)

LCL shipping allows multiple shipments to share container space, making it a cost-effective option for smaller cargo volumes.

Sea freight services typically include:

- Import and export shipping
- Container booking
- Cargo consolidation
- Port handling
- Customs clearance
- Door-to-door delivery
- Shipment tracking
