## What Project Cargo Means in Military and Defense Logistics

Project cargo refers to the movement of heavy, oversized, high-value, or complex equipment that cannot be transported through standard freight methods. These shipments require detailed planning, engineered packing, route feasibility checks, special permits, and supervision at every stage.

In military and defense logistics, project cargo can include armored vehicles, radar systems, communication equipment, generators, turbines, oilfield skids, and other mission-critical assets. Each move demands strict compliance with export regulations, customs procedures, and security protocols.

## Secure Handling Methods for Sensitive Equipment

Security and protection are the foundation of military project cargo. Sensitive equipment must be protected from vibration, moisture, impact, and unauthorized access throughout the journey.

Engineered wooden crating, anti-shock packing, moisture control barriers, and reinforced lashing are used to safeguard cargo. Route surveys are conducted to evaluate bridge clearances, road strength, and environmental risks. <Link href="/services/warehouse-storage-services-dubai">Secure warehouses</Link> with access control and monitoring systems are used before and after transit.
